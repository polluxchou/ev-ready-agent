# Buying Motor Insurance for SC-01 — Nassau County, NY

**Scope:** Owner is a Nassau County (Long Island) homeowner, vehicle is already titled, registered, NY-plated, fully compliant. Only the act of **purchasing motor insurance** is covered here.

## 1. Statutory minimum coverage (NY State, 2026)

Every NY-registered vehicle must carry:

- **Bodily Injury Liability:** $25,000 / $50,000 ("25/50") — per person / per accident
- **Property Damage Liability:** $10,000 ("10")
- **No-Fault / Personal Injury Protection (PIP):** $50,000 per person — covers medical, lost wages, regardless of fault
- **Uninsured / Underinsured Motorist (UM/UIM):** matching 25/50 bodily injury limits — mandatory in NY
- **Statutory death benefit:** $2,000

For an ~$80k EV these statutory minimums are dangerously low. Typical Nassau owners carry **100/300/100** liability + **$250k–$500k PIP / Add-on PIP (APIP)** + **$1M umbrella**.

Source: [NY DMV Insurance Requirements](https://dmv.ny.gov/insurance/insurance-requirements), [NY DFS Auto Insurance](https://www.dfs.ny.gov/consumers/auto_insurance).

## 2. Optional add-ons (recommended for SC-01)

- **Collision** (own-vehicle crash damage) — essential, $500–$1,000 deductible
- **Comprehensive** (theft, fire, flood, vandalism, hail, animal) — essential on Long Island
- **GAP / Loan-Lease Payoff** — critical: imports depreciate fast in year 1
- **Towing & Labor** + **Rental Reimbursement** (~$50/day)
- **OEM Parts Endorsement** — forces use of original parts (matters for an import where aftermarket parts don't exist)
- **Agreed Value** endorsement — only via specialty insurers; replaces ACV

## 3. Documents required to bind a policy

- NY Driver License (Class D) or NY non-driver ID — must match garaging address
- **17-digit VIN** of the SC-01 (may need to be manually input — see §6)
- **NY Certificate of Title** (MV-999) or copy
- **NY Registration (MV-82)** showing plate number
- **Odometer reading** (HSMV-style, at quote)
- **Garaging address** — physical Nassau ZIP (e.g., 11758 Massapequa, 11030 Manhasset, 11550 Hempstead). Cannot be a P.O. box.
- **Prior insurance declaration page** (5-year lookback) — protects continuous-coverage discount
- MVR (motor vehicle record) consent — carrier pulls automatically

## 4. The FS-20 ID card and the DMV timing trap

NY uses the **FS-20 New York State Insurance Identification Card** (paper, two identical parts, with 2D bar code per 15 NYCRR §32.9). The carrier also files an **electronic IIES notification** with the DMV.

**Critical timing:** *In NY you cannot register a vehicle without active NY insurance, and you cannot get a valid FS-20 without a registered VIN.* The clean sequence:

1. Get a **quote bound to the VIN** (insurer issues FS-20 dated within 180 days)
2. Bring FS-20 to DMV → register & plate
3. Insurer transmits IIES electronic confirmation within 30 days

**Since this scenario stipulates the car is already plated**, step 2 is done — but if you lapse coverage even 1 day, DMV auto-suspends registration (and your DL after 31 days). You must surrender plates before cancelling.

Source: [NY Sample Insurance ID Cards (FS-20)](https://dmv.ny.gov/insurance/sample-id-cards), [15 NYCRR §32.9](https://www.law.cornell.edu/regulations/new-york/15-NYCRR-32.9).

## 5. Typical premium range for $80k import EV in Nassau

- NY State average full-coverage premium: **~$2,370/yr** (all vehicles)
- Nassau County (11758 Massapequa, 11030 Manhasset, 11550 Hempstead) runs **15–25% above state mean** due to density/theft
- For an ~$80k imported performance EV, 35–55-year-old clean-record owner: expect **$3,800 – $6,500/year** with standard carriers (GEICO, Progressive, Allstate, Liberty Mutual)
- With a specialty carrier (Hagerty, Grundy, Chubb Masterpiece) on an **agreed-value / limited-use** policy: **$1,800 – $3,200/yr** — but mileage capped (typically 5,000–7,500/yr) and a daily driver must be in the household

## 6. Common pitfalls — the China-import problem

- **No ISO VIN-decoder record.** Chinese-market VINs aren't in ISO/AAIS databases used by GEICO, Progressive, State Farm, Allstate. Quoting engines reject the VIN or default to "Other Make/Model" → underwriter referral → often **declined**.
- **Workaround path A — Specialty / collector carriers:** Hagerty (1980-and-newer "Exotic & Import" program, 10+ yrs licensed, must own a daily driver, clean 3-yr record); Grundy (agreed-value, unlimited mileage option); Chubb Masterpiece (high-net-worth, ≥$50k vehicles).
- **Workaround path B — Surplus-lines E&S brokers:** specialty brokers can place at Lloyd's of London or Markel Specialty using a manual rate filing. Costs more, takes 5–10 business days.
- **Workaround path C — NYAIP** (NY Automobile Insurance Plan / assigned risk) as last resort — expensive, minimums only.
- **Garaging integrity:** registering at parents' Queens address while parking in the Hamptons is fraud — voids the policy at claim.

Sources: [Hagerty Newer/Exotic Eligibility](https://www.hagerty.com/insurance/classic-car-insurance/does-my-vehicle-qualify/newer-vehicles), [NY Auto Insurers Directory (DFS)](https://www.dfs.ny.gov/consumers/auto_insurance/telephone_numbers_and_websites_of_auto_insurers).

## 7. Typical timeline (already-registered vehicle)

| Step | Time |
|---|---|
| Online quote with standard carrier | 10 min (often blocked at VIN entry) |
| Specialty quote (Hagerty/Grundy phone or web) | 24–48 h (underwriter review) |
| E&S surplus-lines placement | 5–10 business days |
| Bind & receive FS-20 PDF | same day once approved |
| First premium payment (down payment or annual) | at bind |
| IIES electronic filing to DMV | within 30 days (automatic) |
| Physical FS-20 cards by mail | 5–10 days |

**Bottom line:** allow **1–2 weeks** from first quote attempt to bound policy for an import. Have prior-insurance dec page, NY title and registration scanned to PDF before starting.
